About NJob AI

Built for modern recruitment consultants who want to work smarter, not harder.

🎯 Our Mission

Traditional job search is manual, slow, and inefficient. Recruiters waste hours copying and pasting job links, comparing descriptions by eye, and writing generic outreach messages. NJob AI automates all of that — letting you focus on the human side of recruitment: building relationships and closing placements.

⚡ Technology Stack

NJob AI is powered by high-speed AI inference models for intelligent matching and relevancy scoring, Puppeteer for live multi-portal scraping across major Indian job platforms, and an Express backend with persistent JSON storage.

🔒 Data Privacy

All client data is stored locally on your own machine in data/clients.json. No data is ever sent to external servers except for the AI API calls required for parsing and scoring. You remain in full control of your clients' information at all times.
